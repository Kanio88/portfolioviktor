Services

One-to-one SEN sessions tailored to every individual.

Every session is designed around the young person’s goals, sensory profile, communication style, and pace — never a one-size-fits-all approach.

01

Autism support sessions

Structured, sensory-aware one-to-one sessions for autistic children and young adults. We work at the individual’s pace, building trust through consistency and predictability.

02

Life skills development

Practical life skills programmes focused on independence — from daily routines and self-care to social confidence and community participation.

03

Communication & social skills

Strength-based coaching to support communication strategies, AAC, and social interaction, aligned with each young person’s goals and natural style.

04

Sensory-informed care

Sessions shaped around sensory sensitivities, using structured environments, visual schedules, and calming strategies to reduce overwhelm and build regulation.

05

Family & carer guidance

Collaborative guidance for parents, carers, and families: sharing strategies, reinforcing progress at home, and building a consistent support network.

06

Personalised support plans

Detailed, person-centred plans developed with input from the individual, family, and relevant professionals — documented and regularly reviewed.
